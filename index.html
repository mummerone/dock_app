Dock App · for LTL dock managers

Every trailer out full, within weight limits and on time

LTL = less-than-truckload: many shippers' freight on one trailer.

  • Every piece gets a slot
  • In the demo, all 5 trailers leave with the floor full (no floor length left, nose to tail) at 76–80% of the weight limit, within the kingpin and rear-axle limits (freight only: trailer weight not counted). Floor-full, not weight-full: normal for LTL.
  • Forklifts never block each other: never two in one trailer at the same time

1 move = one forklift trip carrying one piece from an inbound trailer to its slot in an outbound trailer. 8 stops = 8 key moments, about 2 minutes; the moves in between play on the map. 100 stops = every single forklift move. Skip to the summary any time. Uses sample freight on 48 ft trailers; your own freight is saved and comes back when you exit.

Words used here: light = 900 lb or less; heavy = over 900 lb · move = one forklift trip carrying one piece · levels = Floor (the trailer floor), Deck 2 (second level) and Deck 3 (third level); Deck 2 and Deck 3 rest on load bars above the piece below · pallet = one piece of freight on a pallet · at target = 95–100% of a limit · tight = still on time, but with under 15 min to spare (if anything slips, hold the trailer a few minutes or start it earlier next shift; adding a forklift won't help that one trailer, because never two forklifts work in one trailer at the same time) · balanced = the heavier support (kingpin or rear axle) carries no more than 25% more than the lighter · kingpin = the pin at the front of the trailer that sits on the tractor; it carries the front of the load · OS&D = Over, Short & Damaged freight · fragile = floor only, nothing stacked on top. Demo trailer: 48 ft, 12 sections of 4 ft, front support = kingpin (at 3 ft), rear = one single axle (at 42 ft). Weights checked at 20,000 lb at the kingpin (a demo limit, same as one axle) and 20,000 lb on the rear axle (the federal single-axle limit, stricter than a 34,000 lb tandem), freight only: the trailer’s own weight is not counted. Weight % = freight ÷ 40,000 lb (both checks added; a demo setting). Every 4 ft section, nose and tail included: 3,200 lb max (company setting). Nose and tail (sections 1 and 12): light pieces only, 900 lb or less. Sections are grouped Nose 1, Front 2–4, Middle 5–8, Rear 9–11, Tail 12. Inside roof height assumed 110 in (stacks stay under 100 in). Sample forklift limit: 5,000 lb per piece.
